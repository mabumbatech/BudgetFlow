Privacy Policy

How MABUMBATECH handles information in BudgetFlow.

What we store

Your name, email address, phone number and password (stored only as a secure hash), plus the financial records you enter: accounts, transactions, debts, budgets, savings goals and recurring items. If you pay for a subscription we also store the payment reference, date, amount and any receipt image you upload.

How we use it

To provide the service: show your balances and reports, send in-app notifications you have enabled, process subscription payments and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use your financial records for advertising.

Who can see it

Your financial records are visible only to you. Administrators can see account details needed to manage subscriptions (name, email, phone, subscription and payment status) but not your private financial records.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions are protected, requests are checked against forgery, uploaded files are stored privately, and sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Cookies and device storage

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. Your device may also store your theme preference and, while you are offline, expenses and income waiting to sync.

Your choices

You can update your details, export all your data, or permanently delete your account and financial records from your profile page.
